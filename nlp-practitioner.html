What you'll learn

Eleven modules, from how people take in information to how they change.

  1. Module 1Foundations of NLPOrigins and history, the LSIPT concept, mirror neurons and where NLP is used day to day.
  2. Module 2The communication modelRepresentational systems (VAKOG), calibration, sub-modalities and language patterns.
  3. Module 3How people filter the worldThe presuppositions of NLP, meta programs, beliefs and values, and how memories shape attitudes.
  4. Module 4The language of influenceThe Meta Model, empowering and disempowering language, and non-verbal communication.
  5. Module 5AnchoringAssociation and dissociation, VAK anchors, stacking and chaining, and verbal anchoring.
  6. Module 6Changing habits and fearsThe swish pattern, double dissociation and the movie-theatre technique.
  7. Module 7Neurological levelsChunking up and down, and moving a conversation between levels.
  8. Module 8ReframingContext and content reframes, six-step reframing, useful frames and behavioural flexibility.
  9. Module 9Mental space and relationshipsMental Space Psychology, social panorama and perceptual positions.

  11. Module 11Metaphor and Clean LanguageEliciting and working with metaphors, integrated with the Meta Model.
