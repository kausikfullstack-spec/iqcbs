Questions people ask first

Do I need a background in psychology?

No. The Associate and Practitioner courses start from first principles, and most participants come from HR, sales, teaching, coaching and management. You can join Practitioner directly.

Is the training online or in person?

Practitioner is hybrid: five full days you can attend in our Gariahat classroom or live online, then three online days. Master Practitioner is residential at Raichak, followed by four online days. Specialties are mostly evening or weekend sessions.

Which certificate will I receive?

Practitioner graduates are certified by the American Board of NLP and the European Community for NLP. To certify you attend every day, score at least 80% on assignments and demonstrate anchoring, the swish pattern and double dissociation. Certification does not expire, so there is nothing to renew.

How do retakes work?

Once you have completed a course, you can join any later cohort of it again, as many times as you like, without paying the course fee. For Master Practitioner retakes you pay only for lodging and boarding at the residential venue. Your certificate never expires, so a retake is for practice and refreshing your skills; a new certificate is not included.

Do the fees include GST? Can I pay in instalments?

Fees shown exclude 18% GST, which is added to your invoice. [INSTALMENT AND REFUND TERMS]

Is this therapy?

No. Our programmes teach coaching, communication and personal-development skills. They are not medical or psychological treatment and do not qualify you as a counsellor or clinical therapist. If you are dealing with a mental-health condition, please see a qualified clinician.
