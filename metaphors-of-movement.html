Metaphors of Movement

Metaphors of Movement studies the metaphors that arise naturally in how people talk, and how they shape reasoning. It focuses on metaphors of movement, or the lack of it, so it is at its most useful with stuck states.

  • Level 1 equips you to run basic content-free MoM sessions
  • Level 2 completes Practitioners status
  • Both levels online, in 3-hour sessions

Syllabus

Two levels

  1. Level 1 · Part aThe MoM process
    • •Full outline of the MoM process and session structure
    • •Neurological rationale for the processes
  2. Level 1 · Part bMetaphor taxonomy and digital change
    • •The structure of outcomes
    • •Obstructions: external rules and values
    • •Containers: container metaphors, inherited status, families and long-term problems
    • •Burdens: responsibilities and guilts
    • •Bubbles: beliefs and values
  3. Level 2 · Part aRelationships and generative change
    • •Elevations: status games and awkward positions
    • •Relational positions: where we stand in relation to others
    • •Fulfilments: emotional trading and transactions
    • •Hurts: emotional injury and emotional health
  4. Level 2 · Part bSymbolism and time in metaphor
    • •Dream work and dream analysis
    • •Introduction to metaphors of time
    • •Introduction to metaphors of business and money

How the levels fit

  • •Level 1, The Foundation: the structure of MoM and the change-work process
  • •Level 2, The Metaphoric Hierarchy: how metaphors lead to new metaphors, with a theme of relationships and emotional health
