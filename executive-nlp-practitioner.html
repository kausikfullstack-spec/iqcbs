Syllabus

The full Practitioners curriculum, in eleven modules

  1. Module 1Foundations of NLP
    • •Origins and history, the LSIPT concept, mirror neurons and where NLP is used day to day
  2. Module 2The communication model
    • •Representational systems (VAKOG), calibration, sub-modalities and language patterns
  3. Module 3How people filter the world
    • •The presuppositions of NLP, meta programs, beliefs and values, and how memories shape attitudes
  4. Module 4The language of influence
    • •The Meta Model, empowering and disempowering language, and non-verbal communication
  5. Module 5Anchoring
    • •Association and dissociation, VAK anchors, stacking and chaining, and verbal anchoring
  6. Module 6Changing habits and fears
    • •The swish pattern, double dissociation and the movie-theatre technique
  7. Module 7Neurological levels
    • •Chunking up and down, and moving a conversation between levels
  8. Module 8Reframing
    • •Context and content reframes, six-step reframing, useful frames and behavioural flexibility
  9. Module 9Mental space and relationships
    • •Mental Space Psychology, social panorama and perceptual positions
  10. Module 10Introduction to hypnosis
    • •Session flow, suggestibility and conversational hypnosis
  11. Module 11Metaphor and Clean Language
    • •Eliciting and working with metaphors, integrated with the Meta Model
