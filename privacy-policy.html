Privacy Policy

Education Privacy Policy

At IQCBS, we are committed to protecting the privacy and security of students, teachers, and all individuals interacting with our educational services. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, store, and protect personal information in compliance with applicable data protection laws.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of personal data:

  • Student and staff details (name, age, contact information, student ID)
  • Academic records, attendance, and assessment data
  • Online activity and engagement through learning platforms
  • Device and browser information for digital services
  • Parental or guardian contact details for emergency purposes

2. How We Use the Information

The collected data is used to:

  • Provide and improve educational services
  • Track academic progress and personalize learning experiences
  • Ensure a safe and secure learning environment
  • Communicate with students, parents, and staff
  • Comply with legal and regulatory requirements

3. Data Protection & Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to safeguard personal information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. Access to personal data is strictly limited to authorized personnel.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell or share personal data with third parties for commercial purposes. However, we may share necessary data with:

  • Educational partners for curriculum development
  • Government agencies when required by law
  • Technology providers for secure learning management systems

5. Rights & Access

Individuals have the right to access, modify, or request deletion of their personal data. Requests can be made by contacting our data protection officer at partha@iqcbs.com.

6. Updates to Policy

We may update this policy periodically to reflect changes in regulations or educational practices. Any updates will be communicated through official channels.

For any concerns or inquiries regarding this policy, please contact us at partha@iqcbs.com.
